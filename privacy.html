X-40 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

What X-40 collects

X-40 does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network connection of its own.

Microphone and audio input

The app asks for microphone and audio-input access only so you can play external instruments or microphones through your effects and record them. Recordings and projects stay on your device (in the app's folder, visible in the Files app) unless you choose to export or share them.

Third-party plug-ins

Audio plug-ins (AUv3) you load are made by third parties and are subject to their own privacy terms.
